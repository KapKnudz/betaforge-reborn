# Persist company evidence independently of analysis

For repeated research on a maintained company list, deterministically acquire and preserve the agreed source material, including original reports and sourced financial inputs, and prepare reusable page-linked report text. This spends acquisition and storage effort upfront in exchange for repeatable analysis, traceable calculations and reuse across runs; the analyst may obtain additional evidence as questions develop. Keep context selection separate: the [Gränges walkthrough](../granges-walkthrough-results.md) found both upfront report loading and sequential tool reading viable, so this decision does not mandate a single exposure method or a storage technology.

Accepted by the user on 2026-11-01. Original page images and explicit calculation tools remain available in either workflow; see the [agreed scope](../retrieval-design.md#agreed-scope).
