# Company memo structure

Write a short owner’s memo that connects business economics, required operating changes, capital needs and shareholder returns. Separate business quality from attractiveness at the dated report price. A strong business can offer an inadequate return; a conditional valuation can remain useful when one input is missing. Keep that limitation beside the conclusion it affects. Follow the canonical [output discipline](../../../analysis-policy.md#output-and-calculation-discipline) and [calculation catalogue](../../../calculations.md).

Use this outline within the existing six sections. It organizes presentation; it adds no mandatory schema fields.

| Existing section | Reader-facing content |
|---|---|
| `thesis` | Open with three to five sentences, about 90 words: business case, principal dependency, scenario return implications, price attractiveness and largest uncertainty. Add a compact fact panel with analysis date, price/date, currency, horizon, ownership scope and selected hurdle. |
| `drivers` | Explain products, customers and how the company earns money. Select three to five earnings and cash drivers. Beside each material forecast, give the compatible historical anchor, operating mechanism, selected magnitude and strongest contrary evidence. Explain whether growth and margin improvements can occur together. |
| `quality` | Show a small operating record and relevant quality diagnostics. Put actuals beside forecasts, with annual/R12 periods, reported/adjusted basis and scope changes explicit. Explain working capital, recurring investment and financing cash needs. Where forecast cash changes debt or dividends, show the compact earnings-to-cash bridge and unresolved residual. |
| `management` | Compare dated, source-linked guidance or targets with compatible actual results. Distinguish direct guidance from related operating commentary and analyst inference. Explain capital allocation, ownership alignment and material dilution through per-share consequences. Keep exact quotations and page evidence in supporting details. |
| `valuation` | Lead with bear/base/bull outcomes and returns. Use compact tables for operating assumptions and the earnings → EV → debt/additional claims → common equity → forecast shares bridge. Show dividends, retained cash, terminal target and present value with dates. Explain the exit multiple and the return mechanism. Put weighted results after individual scenarios. |
| `risks` | Name two or three observations that would weaken the thesis, their operating or cash mechanisms and the next evidence to inspect. Include one or two coherent disappointment checks that test the principal dependency and funding needs. State what would change the view. |

Keep observations, interpretations and assumptions distinct using the existing paragraph kinds. Pricing power, competitive protection and management quality are judgments requiring evidence. Preserve saved source, observation, assumption and calculation IDs. Follow the [report contract](../../../../betaforge/analysis_report.py), especially `SECTIONS` and `validate_report`; presentation changes must retain traceability.

Explain every chart’s percentage basis, price date, valuation date, target precision, horizon, dividend treatment and scale. Terminal-price change excludes separately paid dividends; discounted value is not an annual return. Do not present individually scaled charts as directly comparable magnitudes.

Explain proposed weights through company-specific outcomes and uncertainty. Precise percentages require reasons; rounding does not validate them. An optional alternative-weight sensitivity can show whether the conclusion depends on small changes. Representative scenario points and omitted tails make weighted values conditional or indicative, rather than complete expected values. Use the [probability and return definitions](../../../analysis-policy.md#probabilities-and-returns): weighted-cash-flow IRR is secondary here, and is not an average of scenario IRRs. A “maintain the current multiple” case is a condition, not independent valuation support; explain separate evidence when claiming the multiple is defensible.

Put detailed formulas, source pages, input IDs, accounting reconciliations, policy identity and revision provenance in a readable appendix. Reuse named calculations; hundreds of calculations or exhaustive prose add no assurance.

The [investor-writing index](../../../research/investor-writing-examples.md) and [memo gap review](../../../research/investment-memo-gap-review.md) explain the editorial basis: [Buffett’s cash and capital explanation](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2007ltr.pdf#page=5), [Munger’s shareholder perspective](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2014ltr.pdf#page=38), and [Pershing Square’s operating chains, slides 41 and 45](https://assets.pershingsquareholdings.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/31170156/2024-Annual-Investor-Presentation-1.pdf). The [design references](../../../../DESIGN.md#references-and-purpose), [NBIM](https://www.nbim.no/en/investments/) and [ABG’s memo](https://abgprivatebanking.se/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Momentum-builds-for-2025e.pdf), inform grouping, fact panels and source notes. These are writing and presentation references, not issuer templates, investor personas or imported trading rules.
