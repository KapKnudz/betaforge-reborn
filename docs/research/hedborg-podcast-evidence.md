## Checked passages

- **Separate earnings growth from rerating.** Hedborg describes a two-to-three-year case with rising sales, improving EBIT margin and a higher multiple if the business attracts more investors. That is a setup he looks for, with a separate mechanism for each part. Betaforge can use the split to test which improvement the projected return requires. A case can also work with stable margins or a lower multiple. [Aktiesnack #1, about 12:04–13:43 in the publisher audio](https://shows.acast.com/aktiesnack/episodes/6298bb4c42c845001359a805)
- **Check which costs scale.** In the new transcript of #116, Hedborg says a wide gross-to-operating margin gap can flag an opportunity, but recurring operating expenses or investment and depreciation may explain why it persists. He also discusses the hypothetical purchase of a business at 6× EBIT by a company valued at 12×. Integration and the quality of the acquired earnings still matter. For a valuation that needs acquisitions, separate organic growth from acquired EBIT and show the cash cost, including contingent payments and funding when material. [Gött Tjöt #116, margin and acquisitions discussions](https://shows.acast.com/nantingomaktier/episodes/116-affarsmodeller-investeringar-och-framgangsrikt-foretagan)
- **Check what an insider trade represents.** If several insiders buy together, Hedborg would first check for a shared incentive program. He gives a second hypothetical example of a CEO who held few shares at a previous company and spends a substantial amount on shares at a new one. Check the register entry and any preferential or incentive terms before inferring a change in alignment. These examples help interpret ownership evidence. They give no basis for a fixed threshold or a return forecast. [Montrosepodden #57, insider buying discussion](https://shows.acast.com/montrosepodden/episodes/57-nastan-allt-du-behover-veta-om-insynshandel-med-modular-f), [Finansinspektionen register](https://www.fi.se/sv/vara-register/insynsregistret)
- **Test the margin against history.** Hedborg gives an unnamed example of a forecast that lifts margin from 10% to 20% after a decade between 8% and 11%. He ties the burden of proof to historical growth and profitability and to the company’s pace of execution. He gives no accounting definition for the margin in this example. [Marknaden video, 18:17–19:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iy7Qdwxstdg&t=1097s)
- **Separate investment demand from the operating thesis.** Hedborg explains why a single institutional name in an ownership register can contain active funds, mandates and index holdings. He describes fund subscriptions and redemptions as sources of trading and daily turnover as a potential eligibility constraint. In a separate hypothetical three-to-five-year case, he gives a low effective float and additional demand as a possible route from 15× to 30× EBIT. He describes a setup he would research, with no observed rerating or general liquidity threshold. [Marknaden video, 46:41–47:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iy7Qdwxstdg&t=2801s), [49:04–51:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iy7Qdwxstdg&t=2944s)
- **Separate ownership changes from financing.** Hedborg says Sprints bought existing Modular Finance shares after the company reached an ARR goal and sought a partner for its next phase. The 40% transaction made Sprints the largest individual shareholder and brought in no new cash for Modular Finance. Hedborg discusses how the founders kept running the business and how differing time horizons could create friction if performance weakened. The issuer confirms the stake and continued founder involvement. The source of the shares and absence of new cash are Hedborg’s account as a founder. [Marknaden video, 56:36–1:00:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iy7Qdwxstdg&t=3396s), [Modular Finance announcement](https://storage.mfn.se/da9db39f-69e8-4696-a3f2-10fa341a57f9/sprints-new-partner-in-modular-finance.pdf)

## Named cases

**Fortnox.** Hedborg recalls following the business for years before buying heavily when Visma withdrew its bid. He attributed part of the sharp fall to sellers leaving a failed deal. The competition authority confirms the 27 June 2016 withdrawal after it sent a draft challenge. The price move, seller motives and trade are his recollection. In a separate 2019 interview he describes room to sell more modules to existing customers. That later explanation helps identify a business driver, while his earlier model remains unknown. He says he sold too early without giving the specific sell trigger. [Aktiesnack #69, 21:01–22:23](https://shows.acast.com/aktiesnack/episodes/69-petter-hedborg-case-trading-och-monsterigenkanning), [Aktiesnack #1, 23:50–25:57](https://shows.acast.com/aktiesnack/episodes/6298bb4c42c845001359a805), [Marknaden 2019 audio, 30:48–33:13](https://media.pod.space/affarsvarldenmagasin/20191205_master_dynamisk.mp3), [Competition authority, p. 56](https://www.konkurrensverket.se/globalassets/dokument/informationsmaterial/rapporter-och-broschyrer/rapportserie/rapport_2017-5.pdf#page=56)

**CellaVision.** He had followed it when he considered it expensive. After two loss-making quarters, he judged the distributors’ weak equipment orders to be a purchasing delay and bought. The issuer’s Q1 2013 report ties weak distributor demand to delays in hospital equipment buying. Its Q3 report shows improving earnings and deliveries of a new product to Sysmex, so the recovery had more than one plausible contributor. His remembered valuation figures vary across the interviews. He says he sold too early. The specific exit trigger remains unknown. [Aktiesnack #69, 22:23–24:10](https://shows.acast.com/aktiesnack/episodes/69-petter-hedborg-case-trading-och-monsterigenkanning), [Aktiesnack #1, 17:48–21:42](https://shows.acast.com/aktiesnack/episodes/6298bb4c42c845001359a805), [Veckans Trade #18, 32:42–37:24](https://play.pod.space/veckanstrade/18-150-000-pa-20-ar-med-petter-hedborg), [CellaVision Q1 2013](https://www.cellavision.com/investors/press-releases/cellavision-ab-interim-report-january-march-2013), [CellaVision Q3 2013](https://www.cellavision.com/investors/press-releases/cellavision-ab-interim-report-january-september-2013)

The reported Q3 figures show how to test the apparent leverage. Year over year, sales rose SEK 9.8m and EBIT rose SEK 6.7m, about 68% incremental EBIT margin. Gross profit rose SEK 4.9m, with a further SEK 1.8m from a smaller gap between gross profit and EBIT. Nine-month EBIT was still below the prior year. A recovery forecast needs to explain which gains can repeat and track whether distributor orders return across subsequent periods. The quarter alone gives no sustainable margin estimate. [CellaVision Q3 2013, Key Ratios](https://www.cellavision.com/investors/press-releases/cellavision-ab-interim-report-january-september-2013)

**Trygga Hem.** He recalls owning the alarm business because spending upfront on installations could create years of subscription receipts. The 2012 annual report confirms that hardware and installation were capitalized, and that new subscribers consumed cash until fees covered the upfront spending. It reports about 4.7 years of payback by the company’s measure and describes financing growth with earnings, credit and equity. The report supports the economics, while his entry date and exit are unspecified. [Aktiesnack #1, 43:18–44:13](https://shows.acast.com/aktiesnack/episodes/6298bb4c42c845001359a805), [Trygga Hem 2012 annual report, Costs and investments](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2013/04/10/537092/0/sv/files/250258/0/Trygga%20Hem%20%C3%85rsredovisning%202012.pdf)

**Söder Sportfiske.** As a customer, he saw knowledgeable staff and a specialist assortment he trusted. He checked the accounts, found a profitable business and approached its owner-operators. He viewed product selection and the ability to advise anglers as advantages against general retailers. The issuer confirms a combined 49% acquisition by Rite Ventures and Hedborg via GrowthCap in 2018. That was a private investment ahead of the 2021 listing. The issuer’s 2021 governance report identifies him as a board member. [Vonheim #115, 06:27–09:18](https://shows.acast.com/bynn-with-christopher-vonheim/episodes/115-petter-hedborg-how-to-find-alpha-in-investing-scaling-mo), [Sparpodden #407, 62:39–67:27](https://open.spotify.com/episode/1luLty202lKqYPUtb0l6Ov), [Issuer history](https://www.sodersportfiske.se/om/om-sodersportfiske/), [2021 annual report](https://www.sodersportfiske.se/documents/2022/soder_sportfiske_arsredovisning_2021.pdf)

**Evolution.** He says he passed because he did not understand iGaming and questioned how well the high margins would hold. He uses it to illustrate how a preference for margin expansion can make an investor overlook a business already earning high margins. This is a decision about his own understanding of the business. [Aktiesnack #69, 56:37–57:05](https://shows.acast.com/aktiesnack/episodes/69-petter-hedborg-case-trading-och-monsterigenkanning)

## What Betaforge can use

- **Explain the path to the margin.** Use the latest comparable result and history to anchor the gross profit and costs needed to reach the forecast. Where scale matters, check distribution, staff, depreciation and any investment needed before the benefit can show up. Keep eventual margin potential separate from what the business can earn in three years. If rerating drives the call, repeat the calculation with a defensible lower multiple and the same business and cash assumptions. [Aktiesnack #69, 54:01–57:05](https://shows.acast.com/aktiesnack/episodes/69-petter-hedborg-case-trading-och-monsterigenkanning), [Vonheim #115, 27:01–28:53](https://shows.acast.com/bynn-with-christopher-vonheim/episodes/115-petter-hedborg-how-to-find-alpha-in-investing-scaling-mo)
- **Name the evidence that would change the case.** CellaVision suggests writing down what the next reports should show if purchases were delayed and what would instead indicate lost demand. Fortnox suggests checking separately whether a price move changes expected earnings, the applicable multiple or the entry price. Trygga Hem suggests testing payback, customer retention and funding before crediting subscription growth with cash.
- **Keep the purchase decision forward-looking.** Hedborg describes updating his estimate after reports or material news and asking whether he would buy at the prevailing price. He gives a broken thesis or a sizeable price overshoot as reasons he might sell a core position. The two named early-sale accounts give no specific rule. Betaforge’s forecasts and scenario probabilities still require target-company evidence. The return hurdle follows the [project policy](../analysis-policy.md#probabilities-and-returns).
