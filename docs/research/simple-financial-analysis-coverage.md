# Existing financial analysis: coverage and minimal improvements

Read-only review, 3 November 2026, of the saved 1 November Gränges walkthrough and current source. No implementation, tests, acquisition, model or valuation runs were performed. Only this findings note was written.

## Overall verdict

The existing foundation can support the requested simpler analysis. Preserve its source storage, period/unit checks, report evidence and current EV/EBIT valuation. The main gaps are a compact recurring financial-quality view, explicit definitions for cash conversion and invested capital, working-capital components, and stock-compensation analysis. Several requested metrics are already acquired but are not presented or definition-checked as reusable analytical outputs. These are narrower gaps than a need for a new valuation architecture. [Storage: `_reports`, `_kpis`, `add_report_facts`](../../betaforge/storage.py#L362); [acquisition plan](../../scripts/fetch_borsdata_context.py#L27); [saved valuation, section 9](../../data/granges-codex/runs/20261101-full/answer.md#9-three-year-operating-valuation).

The large operation count is partly an execution and presentation problem. The principal model uses volume, EBIT per tonne, a multiple, retained cash and an equity bridge. The 326 records also include 82 million-SEK display conversions, a unit probe, sensitivities, reconciliations and superseded pension-free bridges. A smaller analytical surface should be accompanied by fewer duplicate agent calculations, rather than merely hiding the existing log. [Calculation guide, Groups and their uses and Which calculations support the final answer?](granges-calculation-guide.md#groups-and-their-uses); [calculator, lines 301–361](../../betaforge/tools.py#L301); [renderer, calculation rows](../../scripts/render_codex_walkthrough.py#L122).

## Business analysis and implementation priorities

**P1 — Preserve the small operating model and make its reasoning explicit.** For Gränges, keep volume/utilization, fabrication pricing/mix and profit per tonne, scrap/material economics, cash tied up in metal/working capital, and reinvestment/returns as the candidate driver set. Verify which three to five matter rather than scoring a large universal checklist. Existing analysis already discusses these drivers, management execution, gross-income ambiguity, cash weakness and invalidation evidence. The gap is concise, repeatable output and explicit evidence-to-assumption links. [Answer sections 3–4,6,9–11](../../data/granges-codex/runs/20261101-full/answer.md); [prompt questions 2–3,11–13](../../data/granges-walkthrough/codex-questions.md#evidence-questions).

**P1 — Add a small recurring quality view from existing data.** Start with EBIT-margin history, a qualified incremental-margin diagnostic, cash-generation bridge and leverage/share-cost note. These address earnings leverage and cash quality without changing target prices. Put the working-capital fact extraction next because it directly addresses the largest unresolved Gränges cash assumption. Verify ROIC before treating it as comparable; do not create a misleading replacement for issuer ROCE. Evidence and missing inputs are listed above.

**P1 — Reduce unnecessary arithmetic work.** Move million-SEK formatting into deterministic display formatting; reuse common facts/discount factors and avoid asking the analyst to rebuild repeated bridges. Present one selected scenario bridge and a few thesis-relevant sensitivities. Keep source checks and historical scratch records available, but mark superseded outputs explicitly. The existing calculator's scale/currency guards and immutable inputs should remain. [Calculation guide](granges-calculation-guide.md#why-the-count-is-large); [calculator](../../betaforge/tools.py#L301); [renderer](../../scripts/render_codex_walkthrough.py#L122).

**P2 — Complete return-quality definitions, then benchmark them.** A consistent ROIC and FCF-yield comparison is useful after taxes, capital and cash claims are aligned. Prefer the company's own history first. Global peer comparisons need business/economic comparability, not a country-only match; a sector average must remain a context observation rather than a company fact. The current store already separates source observations and contextual datasets, providing a useful foundation without requiring a new valuation model. [Storage schema, observations and datasets](../../betaforge/storage.py#L122); [storage specification, operating quality and aggregation](borsdata-retrieval-storage.md#which-financials-the-analyst-should-see).

**P2 — Reduce reliance on EBITDA.** Retain the existing historical EBITDA data and source reconciliations, but make its valuation cross-check optional. It shares the main forecasts and does not resolve maintenance-capex or cash-conversion uncertainty. EBITDA can still describe a lender/issuer covenant convention when appropriately labeled. [Answer section 9, EBITDA cross-check](../../data/granges-codex/runs/20261101-full/answer.md#ebitda-cross-check); [Q3 definitions, p30](../../data/granges-codex/packet-20261101/reports/sources/granges-q3-2026.pdf#page=30).

## Larger claims not established

This review supports a minimal enhancement plan for the existing Gränges prototype. It does not establish reliable financial definitions for every issuer, a general quality score, a justified discount rate, peer selection, predictive scenario probabilities, or the agreed benchmark return. The saved philosophy review identifies the incomplete versioned philosophy input, return attribution and Investor B hurdle separately. Those gaps should be addressed explicitly rather than obscured by adding more ratios.
