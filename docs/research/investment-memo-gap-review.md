# Investment memo gap review

Reviewed 4 November 2026. This is a bounded editorial and fundamental-evidence review of one preserved company analysis. It does not approve its valuation or establish that the same gaps occur across companies. Exact issuer findings remain in `data/company-memo/20261104/fundamental-review.md`, under ignored private storage. No valuation, provider fetch or new analysis run was performed.

## Recommendation

Use a compact owner’s memo: decision and dated price; business economics; what must improve; earnings and cash; valuation; what would change the view. Put the saved calculations, source differences, management quotations and execution history in readable supporting details. The current report contract already requires six substantive sections and saved references; a new financial method is unnecessary. [Report contract](../../betaforge/analysis_report.py), `REPORT_CONTRACT`; [policy, Output and calculation discipline](../analysis-policy.md#output-and-calculation-discipline).

The substantive priority is evidence connecting the chosen operating outcome to the capital and cash it needs. Attractive direction, spare capacity and a historical peak do not by themselves establish a selected forecast. Keep an unresolved input beside the conclusion it limits. This is the existing requirement in [Valuation methods](../analysis-policy.md#valuation-methods) and [Financial quality](../analysis-policy.md#financial-quality), rather than a new investor checklist.

## Findings to apply cautiously

1. **Present, but difficult to find:** business mechanics, own history, selected quality diagnostics, capital allocation, claims and disconfirming observations already have places in the saved report. The proposed memo should make that material easier to follow. The bounded claim concerns the reviewed artifact; further company examples are needed before changing the generic schema. Evidence: private review, findings 1, 4 and 7; [analysis workflow, Run one lead analyst](../analysis-workflow.md#2-run-one-lead-analyst).
2. **Unresolved support:** some selected operating and cash outcomes have a stated mechanism but lack a sufficiently complete bridge from compatible evidence to magnitude. First show the anchor, required change, capital needed and strongest contrary evidence together. A persuasive rewrite alone cannot close the gap. Evidence: private review, findings 2–3; [policy, Valuation methods](../analysis-policy.md#valuation-methods); Buffett and Pershing examples above.
3. **Known evidence gaps:** distinguish unavailable inputs from missing presentation. Retain explicit limits on cash scope, comparable multiples, claims and ownership. Do not fill them with zeros, introduce a broad ratio scorecard or call group cash owner earnings. Evidence: private review, findings 3 and 5–7; [policy, Financial quality](../analysis-policy.md#financial-quality) and [Evidence and comparisons](../analysis-policy.md#evidence-and-comparisons).
4. **Present, conditional tests:** monitoring and disappointment checks should expose the main dependency, including its funding effect. One small change does not establish that a favorable starting case is conservative. Evidence: private review, finding 8; [policy, Valuation methods](../analysis-policy.md#valuation-methods).

5. **One confirmed source-link defect:** a sampled prose reference points to the wrong report page while the underlying saved observation identifies the correct page. Use the bound observation when preparing the memo. This does not establish a wider mapping failure; full citation accuracy was not audited. Evidence: private review, finding 9; [policy, Output and calculation discipline](../analysis-policy.md#output-and-calculation-discipline).

Overall: proceed with the compact memo and clearer explanations. Keep the reviewed financial assumptions open to challenge. A later evidence-led revision should follow the existing [independent challenge and single-revision process](../analysis-workflow.md#3-challenge-the-draft-independently), with the original report and policy identity preserved.
