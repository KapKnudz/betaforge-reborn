# How to approach the investment hurdle

Reviewed 9 November 2026. Recommendation for discussion. The adopted policy remains a 10% personal annual equity-return requirement, with 9% present-value sensitivity and explained overrides.

## Recommendation

Review the personal hurdle across the portfolio periodically, for example annually or after a material change in available investment alternatives or personal requirements. Set it before evaluating a particular stock. Retain a dated rate and reason in each analysis so a later policy change does not rewrite a historical call. The existing stored required-return input supports this without a new rate engine.

The rate answers how much return the investor requires. The scenario return answers what that stock's specified cash flows would deliver at the purchase price. The current SEK comparison uses nominal cash flows before personal taxes and trading fees. Changing only the required return changes the discounted entry value and the hurdle comparison; it does not change the scenario IRR. A stock that passes the hurdle still needs acceptable downside and assumptions.

For a review, consider dated SEK government-bond yields at a relevant maturity, inflation expectations and credible alternative investments on compatible currency, nominal/real and tax/fee bases. A bond held to maturity differs from a volatile stock or index. Historical SIXRX returns remain historical context. Any expected index return would need its own sourced forward assumptions.

## Why avoid automatic company scores

Weak margins, debt refinancing, working-capital demands and dilution should first enter the operating cases and equity bridge. Lowering a required return because management is good, while also forecasting better margins and a higher exit multiple, can reward the same judgment repeatedly. Raising it automatically for a risk already reflected in pessimistic cash flows can also double count. This is a consistency check, not a claim that probability-weighted cash flows require no risk premium. [Damodaran, investment analysis CT 8.6](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/cfin2E/support/critthink.htm).

A company-specific override can remain a stated investment preference. Name the reason and show the result at the normal hurdle too. Avoid an invented rule such as adding a fixed percentage point for each red flag. A CAPM cost-of-equity estimate would introduce risk-free rates, beta and equity risk premiums with their own estimation choices. It would answer a different question from the personal requirement and is unnecessary for the current workflow.

## What could become dynamic later

If rate changes repeatedly alter decisions, a separately dated comparison such as a relevant SEK government yield plus an explicitly chosen equity-return premium could inform the periodic review. The premium would still require judgment. Choose the source, maturity, observation date and review rule before implementing it. No numerical premium is justified by the current project evidence.

Buffett notes that intrinsic-value estimates change with both cash-flow estimates and interest rates. That supports revisiting valuation inputs as circumstances change; it does not select a universal personal hurdle. [Berkshire 1994 letter, intrinsic value](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1994.html).

RBA research describes several reasons corporate hurdle rates can differ from modeled capital costs, including uncertainty and the value of waiting. Its evidence concerns corporate investment decisions and does not establish a rate for this personal portfolio. [RBA, December 2021](https://www.rba.gov.au/publications/bulletin/2021/dec/why-are-investment-hurdle-rates-so-sticky.html).
