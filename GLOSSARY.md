# Betaforge valuation

Terms used when assessing a company and interpreting its financial history.

## Language

**Current valuation**:
An assessment of a company's value using information available at the time of the analysis.

**Historical valuation context**:
Evidence about how a company's valuation has changed over time, considered alongside its financial history and reports to help explain its current valuation. This does not by itself require reconstructing an analysis using only information available on a past date.

**Reported operating profit**:
Operating profit presented in the company's financial statements, before any separate management adjustment bridge.

**Adjusted operating profit**:
Operating profit after the company's specified exclusions or adjustments. Its definition and reconciliation belong to the company and reporting period.

**Financial observation**:
A value stated by a particular source for a specified company or instrument, period or date, unit and financial definition. Two sources can make different observations of a similar metric.

**Reconciled figure**:
A financial observation checked against identified report evidence for the same period, scope, unit and definition. Reconciliation of one figure does not establish equivalence for every period or company.

**Provider free cash flow**:
The value Börsdata labels free cash flow, retained with its provider definition. It is distinct from a valuation model's explicitly constructed cash flow to the firm or to equity.

**Scenario valuation**:
An assessment of value conditional on a consistent set of business, financing and market assumptions over a stated horizon.

**Future price target**:
An estimated share value at a stated future date under specified assumptions. It is distinct from the present value of that future outcome.

**Present value**:
The value today of future proceeds after discounting them at a specified required return, with the timing and treatment of distributions stated.

**Reverse DCF**:
A discounted cash-flow analysis that starts with an observed price and solves for operating or cash-flow assumptions consistent with that price, while stating the other assumptions held fixed.

**Market-implied expectations**:
Operating or financial outcomes consistent with an observed price under an explicit valuation model. They depend on the other model assumptions and do not identify a unique market forecast.

**Scenario probability**:
An estimated likelihood of a defined outcome over a specified horizon. It is distinct from confidence in the evidence or valuation model.

**Probability-weighted value**:
The sum of comparable scenario values multiplied by their respective probabilities. Values must refer to the same valuation date, currency and claim on the business.

**IRR of expected cash flows**:
The annual discount rate that equates the starting investment price with probability-weighted future payments at their stated dates. It summarizes the selected scenarios and differs from the average of their individual IRRs.

**Return hurdle**:
The return a candidate investment must exceed to qualify for consideration. A hurdle is a decision criterion, not a forecast of the candidate's return.

**Required return**:
The annual return used to discount future proceeds or judge whether an investment is attractive. Its relationship to the investment hurdle must be stated explicitly.

**Group cash conversion**:
Cash remaining from statutory group operating cash flow after cash capex, cash interest outside operating cash flow and lease principal outside operating cash flow, divided by consolidated group net income before distributions. It is distinct from parent-owner free cash flow and issuer-defined cash-conversion measures.

**Operating ROIC**:
After-tax operating profit using an explicit operating-tax assumption, divided by average opening and closing invested capital. Its capital and tax conventions remain distinct from provider ROIC and issuer ROCE.

**Invested capital**:
The selected book capital supporting operations: group equity plus gross debt including leases and additional net pension liabilities, less selected cash and identified non-operating assets. Goodwill and minority equity already included in group equity are not added again.

**Parent-owner free cash flow**:
Cash attributable to the parent's common shareholders after operating, reinvestment and financing needs, before common distributions. Consolidated group cash flow does not establish this amount without accounting for other ownership claims.

**Incremental EBIT margin**:
The change in operating profit divided by the change in revenue over comparable periods. It describes the earnings sensitivity of that observed change, with scope, price effects and denominator stability considered.
